Privacy Policy

Travis Claypool (“we,” “us”) operates the Reindeer Games score updates messaging program.

Information We Collect

When you text HO HO HO to opt in, we collect your mobile phone number and any messages or photos you send to the program number. We may also collect basic message metadata (such as timestamps) necessary to deliver the service.

How We Use Information

We use this information solely to operate the Reindeer Games score tracking program, including receiving game submissions and sending score tallies and related game messages to participants who have opted in.

SMS Opt-In Data

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain message data only as long as needed for the current game season and then delete it.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to Travis Claypool via the program number or through the contact form at travisclaypool.com.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be posted at travisclaypool.com/privacy.
